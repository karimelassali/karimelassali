### 🚀 What I'm Building Right Now

#### 🎓 [Aula](https://aula-platform.vercel.app/) — the classroom platform for Italian schools
Google Classroom is too complex and scatters students across too many places. **Aula brings everything into one simple place** for students, teachers and school directors: classes, auto-graded quizzes, grades, announcements, real-time chat, a social feed, and XP gamification with badges — GDPR-by-design (parental consent, audit log, encrypted database), multi-school from day one.
**Stack:** Next.js 16 · React 19 · TypeScript · Prisma · PostgreSQL
▶️ **Live demo:** https://aula-platform.vercel.app/

#### 🛂 [Soggiorno Track](https://github.com/karimelassali/permesso-di-soggiorno-tracker) — Permesso di Soggiorno tracker
An offline-first Android app that helps foreign residents in Italy track their residence-permit status across both official portals (Portale Immigrazione & Questura) in one place: background status checks, a document vault, an AI advisor, and full Arabic/Italian localisation — backed by an active community on TikTok ([@soggiorno.track](https://www.tiktok.com/@soggiorno.track)).
**Stack:** Kotlin · Jetpack Compose · Room · WorkManager
📦 **Repo:** https://github.com/karimelassali/permesso-di-soggiorno-tracker

#### 🥩 In production for a real client — [Baraka](https://barakasrl.it)
Business website with an AI chatbot widget for Macelleria Baraka, a halal food retailer in Castel San Giovanni (PC) — live and serving real customers.
**Stack:** Next.js · JavaScript · Supabase
🌐 **Live:** https://barakasrl.it · 📦 **Repo:** https://github.com/karimelassali/baraka
